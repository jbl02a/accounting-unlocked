# Curriculum

17 levels in 5 phases. Every level is unlocked from the start — order is a
suggestion, not a gate. Each has a lesson phase, an optional practice phase, and a
"skip the practice, mark the lesson read" escape.

## Phase 1 — Foundations
| # | Level | Teaches | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Accounting Equation | A = L + E | Sort items onto a balance seesaw |
| 2 | The Big 5 Account Types | Asset / liability / equity / revenue / expense | Card sort into five buckets |
| 3 | Debits & Credits | T-accounts, normal balances, DEALER | 10 debit-or-credit questions |
| 4 | Journal Entries | Entry format, debits first | Build 3 entries from dropdowns |
| 5 | Reading a Balance Sheet | Statement structure | Find 3 planted errors |

## Phase 2 — The Accounting Cycle
| # | Level | Teaches | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 | Receivables vs. Payables | A/R vs A/P, "on account", notes vs accounts | 10 MC questions |
| 7 | Compound Journal Entries | Multi-line entries | Entry builder with live totals; won't accept an unbalanced entry |
| 8 | T-Accounts & Ledger Balances | Posting, footing, normal balance side | Foot 6 accounts |
| 9 | The Trial Balance | Column, order, totals | 3 steps: columns → order → totals |
| 10 | Full Cycle Challenge | Capstone | 4 steps: journalize → post → trial balance → analyze |

## Phase 3 — The Why Behind the Rules
| # | Level | Teaches | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 11 | Assumptions & Principles | 4 assumptions + 4 principles, qualitative characteriztics | Matching game + 9 scenarios |

## Phase 4 — Adjusting & Closing (Chapter 3)
| # | Level | Teaches | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | Accrual Accounting & Why We Adjust | Cash vs accrual, the 3 rules, 4 adjustment types | 6 period questions + 6 sorting |
| 13 | Deferrals | Prepaid, supplies, unearned, depreciation, contra accounts | 4 entries, each computed not read |
| 14 | Accruals | Accrued revenue/expense, interest | 4 entries + 4 "what if you forgot" |
| 15 | Closing Entries | Temporary vs permanent, the 3 entries, post-closing TB | 3 steps |

## Phase 5 — The Financial Statements (Chapter 1)
| # | Level | Teaches | Practice |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 | The Four Financial Statements | Preparation order, articulation, single vs multiple-step | 4 steps incl. equation algebra |
| 17 | The Classified Balance Sheet | Current vs long-term, working capital, current ratio | 3 steps |

Levels 16 and 17 share one company (Lakeside Outfitters) so the retained earnings
figure computed in 16 is what makes 17 balance.

## Other pages

- `/exam` — 79 questions across 8 topics. Full exam, random 15, single topic, a
  drill of only what was missed, or a ~24-question **focus test** built from the
  topics the student is under 80% on. Exam mode or instant-feedback practice mode.
  Single-topic drills and the focus test also draw on 44 extra questions in
  `reinforceBank.js`; the full exam and the Quick 15 do not.
- `/cheatsheet` — 14 sections, ~130 terms. Reference, not revision.
- `/cram` — condensed, prints to exactly 2 pages.
- `/worksheet` — printable full-cycle problem in the professor's layout, with key.
